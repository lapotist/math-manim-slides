# Contributing

Contributions should preserve the slow, visual, source-verifiable teaching
standard in `AGENTS.md`.

By submitting project-original code, text, or artwork, contributors confirm
that they control the required rights and agree to dedicate that contribution
under CC0-1.0. Disclose every third-party or adapted input in `SOURCES.md` and
do not add it to the CC0 allowlist without a rights review.

Each lesson contribution needs:

- stable catalog and scene IDs;
- exact source and solution locators;
- deliberate Manim Slides beats and a matching Traditional Chinese presenter
  script;
- an independent mathematical check;
- a draft render and visual review; and
- explicit provenance, permission, and generated-output status.

Never submit private permission correspondence, credentials, personal data,
bulk source downloads, dependency environments, Manim caches, or unreviewed
generated media.
